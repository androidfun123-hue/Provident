New to CareShield Life? Key terms explained

Who's covered, and when you pay

CareShield Life is Singapore's national long-term care insurance scheme.
Born 1980 or laterAuto-covered from age 30
Born 1970–1979Mostly auto-enrolled via ElderShield
Born 1979 or earlierOptional — can opt in
Premiums payableFrom joining age to 67
Premiums are set once, at the age you join, and stay level from then on (aside from scheme-wide reviews). Joining later means a higher level premium, because there are fewer years to fund the same lifetime cover. If you join at 59 or older, premiums are only payable for 10 years instead.

What you get

A monthly cash payout for life if you become severely disabled.
Payout triggerUnable to do 3 of 6 daily tasks
Daily tasks (ADLs)Wash, dress, eat, toilet, walk, transfer
How long it's paidFor as long as you remain disabled
Paid viaMediSave, cash, or family top-ups
The payout amount is set by the scheme each year and rises over time to help keep up with care costs — it isn't affected by your age or how long you've been paying premiums.

Sources & how we worked this out

Enrolment rules, premium payment period, and payout mechanics: CPF Board's CareShield Life pages and premiums and subsidies page. Income-based subsidy tiers (30%/25%/20% of premium for Singapore Citizens with monthly household income per person of $1,500, $1,501–2,600 and $2,601–3,600 respectively; half those rates for Permanent Residents) are also from that CPF Board page; subsidies are further affected by your home's annual value, which isn't captured here. The $206 (male) / $254 (female) reference premium for joining at age 30 comes from MOH's 2026 CareShield Life premium figures, consistent with MOH's original 2020 launch figures ($206 / $253) for the same entry age — the one point in the age range with a clearly published, consistent source. We deliberately do not show a projected premium for other entry ages: MOH does not publish a full rate table, and an earlier attempt by a third-party site to fill those gaps with an extrapolated table was pulled down after being flagged as misleading. The 2026–2030 payout schedule and the ~4%/year growth rate are from the CareShield Life Council's 2025 review, accepted by the Government. CareShield Life Supplement premiums are from the government's official comparison of supplement plans (Sep 2025 edition), for entry age 30 and a $600/month benefit tier only. The Singlife CareShield Supplement premium lookup (Standard and Plus plans, paying to age 69 or 99, at $1,000/$1,500/$2,000 monthly benefit or the maximum benefit payable within $600/year) uses Singlife's own online direct-channel indicative rates, collected 17 Oct 2026, by age next birthday (31–65, gender-differentiated); the $600/year MediSave offset reflects MediSave's current annual CareShield Life Supplement withdrawal limit.
