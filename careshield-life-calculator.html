CareShield Life in brief

Singapore's national long-term care insurance. It pays you a monthly cash payout for life if you become severely disabled.

Who's covered

Born 1980 or laterCovered automatically from age 30
Born 1979 or earlierOptional (most born 1970–79 moved over from ElderShield)
PremiumsPaid yearly until age 67, payable by MediSave
Premium if joining at 30$206/yr male, $254/yr female

What it pays

WhenYou can't do 3 of 6 daily tasks (wash, dress, eat, toilet, walk, move from bed to chair)
How longFor life, while you stay disabled
2026 payout$689/month
Rising to$806/month by 2030 (about 4% a year)
Want more than the base payout? CareShield Life covers part of long-term care costs. A private supplement adds a higher monthly payout on top.

Why add a supplement

CareShield Life is a base. A supplement tops it up.

CareShield Life pays $689 a month in 2026 if you become severely disabled. A Singlife CareShield supplement adds a further monthly cash payout on top, so you and your family have more to work with for care.

Sources & how we worked this out

Enrolment rules, premium payment period, and payout mechanics: CPF Board's CareShield Life pages and premiums and subsidies page. The $206 (male) / $254 (female) reference premium for joining at age 30 comes from MOH's 2026 CareShield Life premium figures, consistent with MOH's original 2020 launch figures ($206 / $253) for the same entry age — the one point in the age range with a clearly published, consistent source. We deliberately do not show a projected premium for other entry ages: MOH does not publish a full rate table, and an earlier attempt by a third-party site to fill those gaps with an extrapolated table was pulled down after being flagged as misleading. The 2026–2030 payout schedule and the ~4%/year growth rate are from the CareShield Life Council's 2025 review, accepted by the Government. The Standard and Plus payout triggers (3 and 2 daily tasks respectively) are from Singlife's Singlife CareShield Standard and Plus brochure. The Singlife CareShield Supplement premium lookup (Standard and Plus plans, paying to age 69 or 99, any monthly benefit from $200 to $5,000) uses Singlife's own online direct-channel indicative rates, collected 17 Oct 2026, by age next birthday (31–65, gender-differentiated); the $600/year MediSave offset reflects MediSave's current annual CareShield Life Supplement withdrawal limit.
