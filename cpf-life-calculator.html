CPF LIFE Retirement Calculator

Key in your age and your current balances (OA and SA if you're under 55, or OA and RA if you're 55 or older). This projects them forward to see whether your Retirement Account reaches the Enhanced Retirement Sum by 65 — and estimates your monthly CPF LIFE payout.

A planning illustration, not a CPF Board figure. This tool is built by Provident Financial Planning for general education and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a product of the CPF Board. It uses simplified assumptions (a single blended contribution and interest rate, rather than CPF's full age-banded contribution and allocation tables) and rough, illustrative adjustments for the Basic and Escalating plans, since CPF Board does not publish those as a simple formula. Your actual CPF LIFE payout depends on prevailing interest rates, your gender, cohort mortality tables and your exact Retirement Account balance at your first payout — none of which this tool can replicate. Always confirm your real numbers with the official CPF LIFE Estimator, and this is not financial advice.

For planning discussion only — not financial advice, and not a CPF Board product. Under 55, this model transfers SA first, then OA, up to that year's projected FRS to form your RA; any leftover stays in OA. At 55 or older, CPF has already done that transfer for you, so the RA balance you enter is used directly instead. If “Top up OA to ERS” is on, leftover OA is used first (at 55, or immediately if you're already 55+), then the model rechecks the gap each later year against that year's rising ERS ceiling. Contribution growth uses the single annual OA/SA contribution amounts you set, held flat in today's dollars — it does not model CPF's age-banded contribution or allocation-rate tables. The monthly payout is estimated by scaling your projected RA against the CPF Board's own 2026-cohort Standard Plan examples at the Basic, Full and Enhanced Retirement Sum levels ($950 / $1,780 / $3,440 respectively), interpolated in between. Deferring past 65 assumes a 7%-per-year compounding increase, consistent with CPF's published deferral bonus; ages 63–64 (marked with the table's earlier rows) are a mirrored extrapolation, since CPF LIFE payouts officially start from 65. Basic and Escalating plan figures apply a rough illustrative adjustment (Escalating starts ~20% lower and grows 2%/yr; Basic starts ~10% lower) since CPF Board does not publish an exact plan-to-plan formula — use the official CPF LIFE Estimator to compare real payout amounts for your plan choice. Reference anchors: CPF Board's published 2026 Retirement Sums and Standard Plan payout examples.
